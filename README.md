# 🏥 Medical Document Intelligence & Patient Timeline

## 📌 Overview

Medical information is often scattered across different documents such as laboratory reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries, medical records, and clinical notes.

Our project, **Medical Document Intelligence & Patient Timeline**, is designed to organize these medical documents and present important patient information in a simple, structured timeline.

The system provides a unified view of a patient's medical history, helping users understand previous visits, diagnoses, medications, laboratory results, and follow-up events.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare information is commonly stored in multiple documents and formats.

Doctors and healthcare staff may need to go through several records to understand a patient's complete medical history.

This can make it difficult to:

- Find important medical information quickly
- Understand the patient's previous history
- Track diagnoses and treatments
- Identify important dates and follow-ups
- Connect information across multiple medical documents

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We developed a web-based **Medical Document Intelligence** prototype that allows users to upload medical documents and view the extracted information in a structured format.

The system converts scattered medical information into an easy-to-understand **Patient Timeline**.
