How this is protected

  • Your record lives under your Patient ID only — a clinician must know that ID and have your consent switched on.
  • Every clinician view is timestamped and logged here, visible to you.
  • Clinicians get read-only access — they can never edit or delete your entries.
  • In production: encryption at rest and in transit, scoped access tokens, audit-grade logging, and HIPAA/BAA-compliant hosting would replace this demo's simplified storage.
